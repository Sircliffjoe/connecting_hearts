1. ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT
The Connecting Hearts for Singles & Married Foundation grew out of the Connecting Hearts Experience, a relationship-building programme founded by Eloho Sodje and launched on July 5, 2025.
The first three editions explored:
This Thing Called Love
Loving You
Love, Sex & Money
Each edition attracted more than 100 attendees and created space for honest conversations, debates, therapy sessions, testimonies, and discussions about the realities of relationships and marriage.
The organization recognized that many people needed more than an occasional conference.
People were dealing with:
Divorce
Grief
Marital conflict
Financial strain in relationships
Emotional and mental health struggles
Single parenthood
Children affected by family breakdown
The Foundation was therefore created to provide ongoing support through three pillars:
Connecting Hearts Experience
Free Counseling & Therapy
Educational Support for Children from Broken and Single-Parent Homes
The 4th edition of the Connecting Hearts Experience, scheduled for August 1, 2026, will also serve as the official launch of the Foundation.
